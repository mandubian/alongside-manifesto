# Announcement — the thread

The launch copy, on the record. Three rules wrote it: never claim
first (claim the gap, not the field); say it's a draft and ask for
disagreement, not applause; the authorship is a fact, not a flex —
acts and credits, never "AI is amazing."

Counts are X-weighted (every URL = 23) and machine-checked by
`tools/tweet-count.mjs` on every push — edit the copy, the check
recounts.
